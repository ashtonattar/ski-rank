# Privacy Policy — Slope Battles

**Last updated:** July 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Slope Battles ("the App," "we," "us") collects, how it's used, and your choices. By creating an account or using the App, you agree to this policy.

*This document is a starting template, not legal advice. It now includes a dedicated CCPA/CPRA section (§5A) written for California residents, but you should still have a lawyer review it before relying on it in production — especially to confirm whether you qualify as a "business" under CCPA's revenue/data-volume thresholds, and if you have users in the EU/UK (GDPR) or anywhere with minors, since those regimes require additional disclosures and mechanisms this template doesn't fully implement.*

## 1. Information We Collect

**Account information.** When you sign up, we collect your email address and password (handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see or store your raw password).

**Profile & content you provide.** Display name, avatar image, games you log (opponents, tricks, results), chat messages sent to other users, friend requests, comments, likes, and any video clips you upload.

**Usage & device data.** Basic technical data generated automatically by our infrastructure provider (e.g., timestamps, error logs) as part of operating the App.

We do not knowingly collect precise geolocation, payment information, or government ID data.

## 2. How We Use Information

- To operate core features: matchmaking, live games, leaderboards, tournaments, friend/chat features, and the Explore video feed.
- To maintain account security and prevent abuse (e.g., spam, cheating, harassment).
- To communicate with you about your account or the App (e.g., game invites, confirmations).
- To improve and troubleshoot the App.

We do not sell your personal information.

## 3. How Information Is Shared

- **With other users, by design.** Your display name, avatar, rating, game history, tournament results, and any clips/comments/messages you post are visible to other users of the App as part of normal functionality (e.g., anything you post publicly in Explore is visible to anyone who can access that feed; friend requests and chats are visible to the people involved).
- **With service providers.** We use Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Storage) to run the App. Firebase processes data on our behalf under Google's own privacy and security terms.
- **For legal reasons.** We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of the App, our users, or the public.
- We do not share your information with third parties for their own advertising or marketing purposes. We do not sell personal information, and we do not "share" it for cross-context behavioral advertising as those terms are defined under the CCPA/CPRA (see §5A).

## 4. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal data within a reasonable period, except where retention is required for legal, security, or dispute-resolution purposes (e.g., preserving records of a reported abuse incident).

## 5. Your Choices & Rights

- You can edit or delete your profile information, messages you've sent, and content you've posted within the App.
- You can request a copy of your data or request account deletion by contacting us (below).
- Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights (access, correction, deletion, portability, objection to processing). We will honor valid requests as required by applicable law.

## 5A. California Privacy Rights (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act ("CCPA/CPRA"), gives you the following rights with respect to your personal information:

- **Right to Know** — You can request that we disclose the categories and specific pieces of personal information we've collected about you, the categories of sources, the business purpose for collecting it, and the categories of third parties (if any) we've disclosed it to, covering the 12 months preceding your request.
- **Right to Delete** — You can request deletion of personal information we've collected from you, subject to certain legal exceptions (e.g., completing a transaction, security, legal compliance, or exercising free speech).
- **Right to Correct** — You can request that we correct inaccurate personal information we maintain about you.
- **Right to Opt Out of Sale/Sharing** — **We do not sell your personal information, and we do not "share" it (as CPRA defines that term, meaning disclosure for cross-context behavioral advertising) with third parties.** Because we don't do either, no "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" link is required, but you may still submit an opt-out preference request at any time and we will honor it going forward.
- **Right to Limit Use of Sensitive Personal Information** — We don't collect Sensitive Personal Information as defined by CPRA (see §1), so there is nothing to limit.
- **Right to Non-Discrimination** — We will not deny you service, charge you a different price, or provide a different level of quality because you exercised any of these rights.
- **Right to Appeal** — If we deny your request, you may appeal by replying to our denial email; we will respond to your appeal within 60 days.

**How to submit a request.** Email **ashtonattar@gmail.com** with the subject line "CCPA Request" and specify whether you're requesting to know, delete, or correct. We will verify your identity using the email address associated with your account (and may ask follow-up questions) before acting on the request. You may also designate an authorized agent to submit a request on your behalf; we may require written proof of that authorization and separately verify your identity.

**Response time.** We will confirm receipt within 10 business days and substantively respond within 45 calendar days, extendable once by another 45 days when reasonably necessary (we'll notify you if that happens).

**Look-back period.** Upon a verified request, we will provide information for the 12-month period preceding the request, unless you ask for information collected before January 1, 2022, and it remains reasonably accessible.

**Shine the Light.** California Civil Code §1798.83 lets California residents ask what personal information we share with third parties for their own direct marketing. We don't share personal information for third-party direct marketing, so no separate disclosure is needed.
